## New wave-4 coverage in `qe-fuzz.mjs` (13 new checks)

- **§16 dt boundary torture** — the exact list `0, -0, 1e-12, .1, ±Infinity, NaN, 'x'` hammered into `fight.step`, `ring.step`, `fists.update`, `protoBoxer.tick`, `tutorial.update` (as `now`), `audio.update` — no throw, outputs in contract, then a real-dt run must still reach a verdict (no phase permanently wedged). Plus a co-sim step where poisoned `dt` is interleaved into a live bout.
- **§17 live mutation + controls** — `FT`/`SPECIALS`/`VSTATS` deep-mutated to negatives/NaN **while a bout runs** → snapshot isolation holds, invariants never break; poison left in place → `reset()` re-sanitizes and the rematch completes. Frozen `opts` (`Object.freeze` before/after construction, `Object.seal`), live `opts.roundLen=NaN`, frozen `sim.t`/`ft.T` rebase. Proto-key battery (`__proto__`/`constructor`/`hasOwnProperty`/`toString`/`valueOf`/`prototype`) into `throwPunch`, `punches[].kind`, `npcVariant`, `protoBoxer.pose/snap`, `trackHands` space, `audio.play/handleEvent`.
- **§18 cross-module co-simulation** — `RingSim+FistTracker+Fight` wired exactly like `game.js` for **10,000 steps**: fists punches → fight inputs, `fight.npcDir()` → ring steering, `ring.distance()` → fight distance. Every step: `hp` monotonic non-increasing within a round (between-round heal legal only on the step `state.round` bumps), `score` non-decreasing, `stamina∈[0,1]` both fighters, `distance` finite ≥0.
- **§19 serialization** — `JSON.stringify(fight.state)` every step of a bout incl. mid-windup telegraph + KO frames → never throws, round-trips data-faithful. **Fields lost on a hypothetical resume** (documented, not a bug — `state` was never a save format): `_rng` PRNG position, `_T/_special/_stat` snapshots, `_phaseT/_actFor/_windupFor/_impacted/_pk` swing internals, `_inp` input buffer, `_tilt/_comboT/_chain/_normals` combo brain, `_prevSway/_prevDuck` edge memory, `_celebrateCd`, `opts`/`_inp` — a resume would need `new Fight(opts)` + `Object.assign(state, snap)` and would re-seed the rng.
- **§19 event-stream invariants** — full bouts incl. forced KOs both directions (`npcHp:25`→npcdown, `playerHp:20`→playerdown): `'ko'` observed only when the pre-step phase was `'fighting'` and post-step phase is `*down`; cumulative `'round-start'` bells `=== state.round` at every observation (legally `round−1` during a round's own countdown since `round++` precedes the bell); game.js-style `splice(0,len)` drain leaves a provably empty second drain; `lastBlow` always `{who∈player|npc, kind∈jab|cross|hook|uppercut|upper, result∈hit|miss|blocked|dodged|whiff, dmg≥0 finite, side∈left|right}`.

## Notes / assumptions

- `punches` proto-kind resolves to `'jab'` post-fix (same fallback policy as every other garbage kind — `lastBlow.kind` stays in the real vocabulary).
- hp "monotonic non-increasing" is asserted **within a round** — the between-round `roundHeal` legitimately raises hp on the step `state.round` increments; that boundary is the one legal climb.
- `opts.ai`/`opts.practice` remain live-read by design (tutorial flips them mid-fight); frozen `opts` simply means the caller opted out of live flips — the sim reads sanitized locals for everything else.
- No git mutations, no `.env`, no new deps, server on :8791 untouched.
