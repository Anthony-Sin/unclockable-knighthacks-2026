HOW A STORY BECOMES SCENERY

A tiny scene specification.

The brief sets moisture, elevation, vegetation, light, and landmark hints. A seeded generator then places the landscape in world coordinates.

There is no remote model call in this demo. Known words shape the scene; unfamiliar words still change its seed and colors, but do not gain a new meaning by magic. A later LLM could produce the same small scene specification, while the browser renderer remains fast and reproducible.
