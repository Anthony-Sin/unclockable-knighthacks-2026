# Fill the Tale — Crystal Caves edition (Variant V4 · trianglify)

One of seven engine variants of the same combined game mockup:
**Forge-Your-Hero × Type-the-Tale**. The kid assembles **Pip**, a crystal-blob
hero, in a hero maker — then types words into story sentences and each word
literally spawns a low-poly prop in the world. Sensible words (bridge, leaf,
unicorn, lantern, key…) help Pip along five story beats. Silly words (banana
peel, anvil, paper bag, toilet…) spawn *real* props too — a peel underfoot, an
anvil that plops into the river and sinks — and then Pip **shatters into his
own triangle shards**, reassembles with dizzy stars, and tries again.

## The engine: trianglify

Every piece of scenery is generated by
[trianglify](https://github.com/qrohlf/trianglify) (v4, loaded from jsDelivr
with a vendored copy `trianglify.bundle.js` as offline fallback):

- **The sky** is one big trianglify mesh (`trianglify({width,height,cellSize,
  variance,seed,xColors}).polys`), each polygon colored by its centroid along
  a vertical two-stop gradient with per-facet jitter.
- **Hills and cave walls** are zigzag silhouettes used as `clipPath`s over
  second trianglify meshes, so the facets keep the low-poly look inside an
  inked outline.
- **The sun/moon and clouds** are small trianglify meshes clipped to discs
  and ellipses.
- **The river** is a trianglify mesh clipped to a wavy band, ramped
  light-to-deep blue.
- **The hero** is triangulated too: boundary + interior points are scattered
  on a soft ellipse and passed to trianglify as `opts.points`; every
  resulting polygon inside the blob becomes a body facet.

If `window.trianglify` fails to load entirely, a hand-rolled jittered grid
fallback still draws triangles so the page never renders blank.

## How the procedural generation works

A **mulberry32** PRNG seeded fresh per call drives all geometry: sun
position, cloud placement, hill peaks, tree/crystal/stalactite/gem placement,
house smoke, and hero point jitter. The world **re-palettes and
re-triangulates per journey area** — Emerald Forest → Misty Rain → Sapphire
River → Amethyst Cave → Golden Home — and **per replay** ("New world, same
hero" re-rolls every seed). The "New world" chip regenerates the current area
mid-game.

**The shatter gag** is the payoff of building the hero from real triangles:
every facet, horn, eye, cheek and foot carries `class="shard"`. On a silly
word, each shard gets a random `translate + rotate` fling and fades out;
after ~800 ms a springy transition class (`.reform`) sends every piece back
to `transform: none` — so the hero literally reassembles out of its own
trianglify polygons while `.dizzy` star-shapes orbit overhead.

## Beats

0. **Forge your hero** — pick facet count (Chunky/Crisp/Sparkly), body hue
   (5 gems), horns/ears (4 styles), eyes (3 styles). "Surprise me" randomizes
   and mini-shatters the preview. The same renderer builds the in-game Pip.
1. **Emerald Forest** — "Pip needs a ___ to cross" → crystal bridge, log,
   vine, plank, stepping stones, leaf-boat.
2. **Misty Rain** — "Pip grabs a ___" → umbrella, leaf, hat, newspaper,
   mushroom; silly hats (paper bag, toast, sieve, spaghetti) droop soggy.
3. **Sapphire River** — "Pip rides the ___ across" → gem-boat, raft, duck,
   fish, or the **crystal unicorn** — Pip visibly mounts and glides across.
4. **Amethyst Cave** — "Pip lights a ___" → lantern, torch, candle, firefly
   cluster, glow gem: a radial glow lifts the dark and the wall gems wake up.
5. **Golden Home** — "Pip opens the ___" → door (swings on its hinge), key
   (flips into the lock), cookie jar (snack first). Letter-gem + word recap.

**Support character:** Hoot, a faceted owl, perches in every area and glides
ahead to the next one during each travel transition.

**Word logic:** hardcoded per-beat `sensible`/`silly` tables (+ plural and
article forgiving). Near-miss a sensible word ("bridg") → a "Did you mean
bridge?" chip that accepts it (edit-distance ≤1, or ≤2 for long words).
Gibberish → gentle "is that a word?" wobble. Unknown-but-wordy words spawn a
mystery crystal chunk, so no typed word is wasted.

**Watch it play** forges a random hero, then types a scripted chapter —
banana peel → bridge → leaf → anvil → unicorn → torch → door — at kid-typing
cadence (with occasional typos it backspaces and fixes). Stoppable any time
via the stop chip, any key, or a tap.

## How the real version would work

The trianglify pipeline is production-shaped already: a session seed from the
backend would make every kid's storybook unique-but-stable, and the
hardcoded prop table would become an asset service keyed by classified word.
A kid-safe word model would label each typed word sensible / silly /
not-a-word (the edit-distance "did you mean" chip is the honest stand-in),
while an adaptive tutor tunes which story beats come next. The animation
contract stays identical: `word → spawned prop + gag`, only the lookup gets
smarter.
